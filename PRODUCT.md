# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Cashiers** at a shop counter in the UAE, ringing up sales quickly with a barcode scanner, searching products, taking cash/card payments, printing or WhatsApp-sending receipts. They work on touchscreen tills and on PCs with mouse and keyboard in equal measure, often under time pressure with a queue.
- **Shop owners and managers** managing products, stock, purchasing, customers, staff, branches and reports in the dashboard, and checking today's sales and profit on their phone (Owner App).
- **Customers** see the customer-facing display, printed/bilingual invoices and the WhatsApp receipt page.

## Product Purpose

MyPosLab is a web-based point of sale for UAE shops: selling, stock, purchasing, customers, accounting and reports in one browser app, plus UAE-specific features (e-invoicing readiness, WhatsApp receipts, batch & expiry, customer display, owner app, multi-branch). Success: a cashier completes a sale in seconds without training, and an owner trusts the numbers.

## Positioning

A modern POS built for the UAE: VAT 5%, TRN, bilingual tax invoices and PINT-AE e-invoice data for the 2027 mandate, WhatsApp receipts and expiry tracking out of the box, without paid add-ons.

## Operating Context

- Shop counters: touchscreen all-in-one terminals and PCs with USB barcode scanners, receipt printers, cash drawers; a second monitor for the customer display.
- Back office on desktop browsers; owners on mobile phones.
- English-first staff UI; Arabic (RTL) must work and is used on customer-facing outputs (invoices, receipts, display).
- First markets: grocery/supermarket, pharmacy, mobile/electronics, fashion/cosmetics.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Laravel 12 + Vue 3 + Tailwind 4; fork of NexoPOS (GPL-3.0). Theming is token-based in `resources/css/{light,dark,phosphor}` and consumed by core and the six `modules/Mpl*` modules through semantic classes.
- Internal identifiers (`nexopos_*` tables, `ns-*` components) stay unchanged for compatibility.
- Feature modules: MplEInvoice, MplWhatsApp, MplBatches, MplCustomerDisplay, MplOwnerApp, MplBranches.

## Brand Commitments

- Name **MyPosLab**; logo is a flask containing a barcode on a cyan→indigo gradient (`public/svg/myposlab-*.svg`).
- The owner explicitly does **not** want a dark-background interface; the default experience must be light.
- Credit to NexoPOS (GPL-3.0) stays in the footer and NOTICE.

## Evidence on Hand

- Demo grocery data ("Al Noor Grocery", AED, VAT 5%) generated by `ns:reset --mode=wipe_plus_grocery`; screenshots in `docs/images/`.
- No real customers, testimonials or pricing exist yet; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Speed at the counter beats decoration: every sale-path action is one tap or one key away.
2. Numbers must be trustworthy and legible: money, VAT and stock are always clear and consistent.
3. Works the same for touch and mouse; nothing depends on hover.
4. English-first, Arabic-ready: RTL and bilingual outputs are first-class.
5. Calm and professional for long shifts: light, low-glare, high contrast where it matters.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for text and controls; touch targets ≥ 44px on the POS; full keyboard operation for scanner/PC users.
